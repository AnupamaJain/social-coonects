**These are scripts, not videos.** You record them. RECORDING-SETUP.md exists so
you don't spend the first hour fighting the demo data — the seed already
contains everything the shots need.

## The one thing to get right

Every script here is built on the same structural bet: **the product demos
itself in under four seconds.** A number on screen changes from red to green
while the viewer watches. That's rare — most SaaS has nothing to film, which is
why most SaaS video is a founder talking over a Notion doc.

Don't narrate the feature. Film the number moving. Say less than feels right.
